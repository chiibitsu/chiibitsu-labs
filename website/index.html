How it runs

Two weeks, and then we are not in the room.

Step 01 Week 0

Map, and measure the baseline

We map one department's real work, measure the workflows as they actually run today, and rank what AI fits — and what it does not.

Step 02 Week 1

The Lab, on your real work

Four hours on site with the people who do the job. Every demo runs on their own workflow — never a sample, never a slide.

Step 03 Week 2 onward

Practice, repair, hand over

Guided practice with a clinic window while it beds in, a before/after review — and then it is theirs. If the workflow is not running live by the end of that window, the Lab runs again at no charge.
